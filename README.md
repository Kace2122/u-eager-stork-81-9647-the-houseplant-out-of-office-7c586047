One out-of-office auto-reply written as if a houseplant wrote it, at most 120 characters.
`main.py` writes the reply to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic, with a 1–120 char self-check).

Entry: "Out of office: I am busy photosynthesizing. I will reply once I have finished drinking the sunlight."
